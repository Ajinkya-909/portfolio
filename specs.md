# Portfolio Website Specification

## Overview

The portfolio should present me as a Software Engineer with a strong backend focus while showcasing my projects, internship experience, technical skills, and ongoing growth.

The primary objective is not to show flashy animations but to communicate:

* Who I am
* What I build
* My technical capabilities
* My experience
* My flagship projects
* How to contact me

# Overall Design Goals

The portfolio should communicate:

1. Strong backend engineering skills.
2. Real-world internship experience.
3. Significant project work (SentinelX as flagship project).
4. Continuous learning and growth.
5. Professional and mature presentation.

The visitor should leave with the impression:

"Ajinkya is a backend-focused software engineer who has built substantial systems, has practical industry experience, and is actively growing toward system design and cloud engineering."
